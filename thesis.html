Master’s Research | 2022

The Impact of Railway Stations Development on Land Price: A Case in West Japan

Research Background

When a new railway station opens, improved accessibility may attract new businesses and increase surrounding land prices. However, this relationship is not necessarily direct.

This research examines how railway station development influences land prices, particularly through the mediating role of business facility density.

Methodology

The study employs Causal Mediation Analysis (CMA) to distinguish the direct relationship between railway station development and land prices from the indirect relationship through business facility density.

Causal mediation analysis framework

Main Findings

Railway station development is positively associated with both land prices and business facility density.

Approximately 17.36% of the estimated total effect is mediated through business facility density. This suggests that changes in surrounding economic activities represent an important pathway linking railway station development to land prices.

Research Implications

The findings provide insights into the relationship between transportation infrastructure, economic activities, and land values, particularly in the context of urban planning, transit-oriented development (TOD), and land value capture.

Limitations

The regression models have R-squared values below 0.15, suggesting that additional explanatory variables are needed to better understand land price changes around railway stations.
